Fix #2830 follow-up: Restrict loop jump target scope to outer block constraints

Follow-up to #2830 to prevent spurious jump collisions across nested scopes.
Because IL offsets can collide and target scopes are local to their method
body, CollectLiveTargets has been restricted to DescendantsOutsideNestedFunctions.
Added a new test case Issue2830_ForLoopWithNestedLambdaTargetConflict that
proves nested lambdas do not block an outer for loop reconstruction.
